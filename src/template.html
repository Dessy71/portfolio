About me

Where engineering
meets storytelling.

I'm Desmond — a developer who designs, and a designer who ships code.

I've just gone full-time freelance, building websites, brand content and automation for clients. Before that, at Ntrakwah & Co. I bridged technology and business development: managing the firm's digital presence, driving engagement through web strategy, content and design, while keeping the office IT running.

I build and maintain responsive, user-friendly websites with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React — keeping accessibility and cross-device behaviour in mind. Git keeps my work tidy; Java and C++ gave me the structured problem-solving habits I lean on every day.

Beyond code I produce branded content — graphics in Canva, motion graphics, and multi-angle podcast shoots edited in CapCut and DaVinci Resolve. That mix is what makes the work land: the site performs, and the story travels.

And because a good site needs a live audience, I also run communities. As General Secretary of SACSU-KNUST I managed the union's online spaces — onboarding, moderation, engagement — and I still build for them today: the registration platform, n8n automations and member CRM that keep it running. More on that below ↓
